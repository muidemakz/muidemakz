01 The short version

I'm a Senior Product Designer

With 6+ years designing payment, wallet, credit, and B2B commerce experiences, I lead end-to-end design at OmniRetail alongside product managers and engineers. My work spans retailer workflows, the shared wallet layer, and the systems that connect a multi-product ecosystem serving 150,000+ retailers. I bring research, interaction design, and systems thinking together to make complex products clearer and more trustworthy.

Beyond design

I'm committed to growing design talent. Through Muidemakz, my independent practice, I mentor junior designers and lead small cross-functional teams shipping production-grade work for early-stage founders. At OmniRetail and Synarktech I've coached designers, facilitated workshops, and championed collaborative practices that elevate both teams and outcomes. For me, leadership is about empowering others to create impact while aligning design with business strategy.

Currently

I own and am building Valideity, an AI-first UX research product that lets teams test a study with simulated personas, then share the same study with real people. It extends my practice into 0→1 product definition and AI-assisted implementation. The central design challenge is making AI useful while keeping predicted results distinct from observed user behavior. Explore the product story →

When I'm not designing or mentoring

I'm probably watching movies, burning calories at the gym, making memories in the kitchen, hanging out with friends, thinking through a strategy game, or taking long walks that spark fresh ideas.
